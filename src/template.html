Your antenna comes first. The flight network fills in what it cannot hear, and is all there is when no receiver is in reach. Flight network data by ADSB.lol.
Web browsers may not read the flight network directly, so it is fetched through a small relay that runs on your receiver. Leave this empty to use the relay on the receiver this page came from.
