# Guidance for agents and code reviewers

Read BUILD-NOTES.md first. It records what is done, what is left in order, the decisions with their reasons, and how to build and test.

## Where to look

- Review the source in src/ and relay/. The root files sky3d.html and sky3d-network.html are generated by tools/build.js from src/; do not review them line by line.
- The page is shared on the internet, so treat every viewer as untrusted. Text from outside (flight data, route services, the search box) must be drawn with textContent, never as HTML.

## Deliberate decisions (please do not re-flag these unless you find a real bug in them)

- Search covers every aircraft the page is tracking, not only aircraft in view. A viewer who searches for a flight wants to find it even when the camera looks elsewhere, and flying to it is the point of the result. Clicking on the sky does require the aircraft on screen.
- The camera does not yet have one explicit owner (tour, follow, fly or lock). The change was kept small on purpose for version 3.6: a follow.tour flag and a leaveControls function. A full rewrite is planned before the watch list and gallery add more camera modes. Flag a concrete bug in the current behavior; do not ask for the rewrite in this pull request.
- Fly mode uses the camera's real heading, the negative of its orbit angle. The tour code still uses the orbit angle directly and was left unchanged.
- Touch input is not designed in yet.

## The LED flight board (board/)

- board/CIRCUITPY runs on CircuitPython 9 or later on an Adafruit Matrix Portal S3, not on a computer: no os.path, no bisect, no thousands separator in format, no str.title. sky/logic.py and sky/web.py must stay free of hardware modules so tools/board_test.py can run them.
- Anything merged to main reaches every board within the hour through the updater. Treat board/CIRCUITPY/manifest.json, sky/logic.py safe_path and plan_update, and boot.py as the safety boundary: a list must never be able to replace boot.py or the owner's config.json, write outside the drive, or leave the board with half an update.
- boot.py is never updated over the air on purpose, so a mistake in it cannot be fixed remotely. Flag anything in it that could fail.
- The board's settings (Wi-Fi password, location) stay on the board in config.json and must never be sent anywhere.
- The lib/ files are Adafruit's, copied unchanged; do not review them.

## Review guidelines

- Point out real bugs, security problems, and regressions in behavior described in BUILD-NOTES.md.
- Prefer findings that can be checked with the simulator (node tools/sim.js 8090) and the checks in tools/controls.js.
- Do not merge. The owner merges.
